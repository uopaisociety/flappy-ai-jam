1. Get the game running

Bring a laptop and charger. Before you arrive, sign in to Gemini with a personal Google account and open Canvas once. Free Gemini is enough. If you only have a phone or can’t sign in, ask at the help desk to pair up.

Start in Gemini Canvas

  1. Choose Copy starter prompt. It includes the whole game.
  2. Open Gemini in your browser and choose Canvas first. Then paste the starter prompt into that chat and send it.
  3. Open Preview and play one round. Once you can flap and restart, you’re ready.

2. Make one change

The host starts the 30-minute clock once everyone is set up. One small change that works is enough to enter.

Send your first change to Gemini

Pick an idea from your course or interests. You could fly through a museum, dodge footballs, or give players a medal at five points.

Make the bird fly through a museum at night. Change only the artwork. Keep Space and tap to flap, and keep the same score and restart.

  1. Send one request in the same Canvas chat.
  2. Play a round after each change. Check that you can flap, score and restart.
  3. Save your last working version. You can hand it in from 19:05, even if you plan to keep experimenting.
Game stopped working or Canvas got stuck?

Describe what happened and ask for one fix. If Gemini cuts off or loses an earlier change, open a fresh Canvas chat and paste your last working HTML. If you hit a usage limit or can’t open Canvas, pair up, ask at the help desk, or try AI Studio Build mode.

Need an idea?

These three example games show how far one change can go. Try one, then look at the request that made it.

3. Hand in your game

From 19:05, bring your game title, team name and main AI request to the help desk, with either your file or a playable link.

  • One HTML file: Bring your finished index.html. In Canvas, copy the full game code into a plain-text file with that name. We’ll play this version directly in the gallery.
  • Multiple files: Bring the live GitHub Pages URL or another public link that opens your game. Test it in a private browser window.

You can also include your original Gemini Canvas share link. In Canvas, choose Share & export → Share → Copy link. The gallery shows your game, team name and AI request.

Submit online with a GitHub account

The form accepts a playable link, pasted single-file HTML, or both. Check the issue afterwards for your gallery link or a note about what to fix.
